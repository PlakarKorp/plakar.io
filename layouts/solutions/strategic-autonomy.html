NIS2 and DORA

Building blocks for your obligations.

No tool makes you compliant on its own. Plakar gives you the technical controls regulators expect around backup, encryption, and recovery.

{{ range (slice (dict "ref" "NIS2, Art. 21" "need" "Backup management and disaster recovery" "how" "SLA policies set frequency and retention per tier. Sync tasks keep a second copy, and check tasks verify every snapshot.") (dict "ref" "NIS2, Art. 21" "need" "Cryptography and encryption" "how" "End-to-end encryption at the source, with independently audited cryptography and keys in your own secret manager.") (dict "ref" "NIS2, Art. 21" "need" "Access control, asset management, and MFA" "how" "Granular roles, two-factor authentication you can require, an audit log, and inventories of what must be protected.") (dict "ref" "DORA, Art. 12" "need" "Backup policies, restoration, and recovery" "how" "Policies show the RPO they deliver. Restore any snapshot to its original location or a separate one.")
