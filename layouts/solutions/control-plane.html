The problem

Backing up one system is solved. Orchestrating the resilience of the entire estate is the hard part.

Plakar protects any single source. The Control Plane is what makes resilience provable, consistent, and governable across all of them.

{{ partial "icon.html" "ri/search-line" }}

No single view of the estate

Every host, team, and cloud runs its own backups. Nothing shows what is protected and what is not, so coverage gaps surface only during an incident.

{{ partial "icon.html" "ri/flow-chart" }}

Policy that does not scale by hand

Retention, schedules, and encryption set resource by resource drift the moment the estate grows to hundreds of sources across clouds and clusters.

{{ partial "icon.html" "ri/shield-keyhole-line" }}

Keys and credentials sprawl

Encryption keys and storage credentials end up scattered across scripts, hosts, and CI, with no central, audited place to manage them.
