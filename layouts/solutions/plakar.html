The problem

Backups fail exactly when you need them most.

{{ partial "icon.html" "ri/spy-line" }}

You have to trust your infrastructure

Legacy tools encrypt at rest, so the backup server, the network, and the storage provider still see your data and hold your keys. Zero-trust can't be an afterthought.

{{ partial "icon.html" "ri/hard-drive-2-line" }}

Encryption kills deduplication

Encrypt with traditional tools and they can no longer deduplicate, so every backup is a full transfer and storage and egress costs balloon.

{{ partial "icon.html" "ri/lock-2-line" }}

Proprietary formats hold you hostage

Closed archives and appliances put a vendor between you and your own data. Your backup should outlive any vendor, cloud, or appliance.

How it works

Your data becomes a Kloset.

Plakar reads from your sources, then chunks, deduplicates, compresses, and encrypts in a single pass. The result is a Kloset: an open, self-contained store of snapshots on storage you own. Keys never leave your machine, and every snapshot is a logical full you can verify.
