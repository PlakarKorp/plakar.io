## Security

- The target of a hard link in an archive is now resolved within the archive. A
  hard link pointing outside it, such as `../../etc/shadow`, could previously
  lead a restore to write outside the target directory.

## Fixes

- Symbolic links in an archive are backed up as symbolic links. They were
  previously backed up as regular files.
