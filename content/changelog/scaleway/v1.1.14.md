## Improvements

- The integration is built on version 1.1.5 of the Plakar integration SDK.

## Fixes

- Temporary snapshots and objects are now deleted even when a backup or restore
  fails or is cancelled.
- Restores now wait for the restored volume to be available, and fail when it
  ends in an error state.
- Fix the wrong modification time on snapshot metadata and secret records.
