## New

- Nova servers can now be restored onto an existing server, with
  `openstack-instance://<server-id>`. Every backed-up disk, including the root
  disk, is restored as a new Cinder volume and attached to that server, and no
  server is created.
  ([#250](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/250))

## Improvements

- The inventory no longer lists Glance images.
  ([#254](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/254))

## Fixes

- Restoring an encrypted volume now reuses the encryption key of the original
  volume. Before, Cinder encrypted the restored data again under a new key, and
  the volume appeared as a raw LUKS device instead of mounting its filesystem.
  Only the key ID is kept in the backup, so the keys themselves have to be
  secured separately to decrypt the volume after a restore.
  ([#244](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/244))

## Security

- The inventory configuration now rejects empty credentials, and restores
  validate the disk IDs read from a backup. Neither gap was exploitable, and
  both align with the hardening already applied elsewhere in the integration.
  ([#240](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/240))
