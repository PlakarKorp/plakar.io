## New

- When plakar runs on a Nova server of the same cloud, project and region,
  volume backups attach the temporary volume to that server and read it as a
  block device, and volume restores write to a new volume attached to it.
  Nothing goes through Glance. Otherwise, or when the attachment fails, the disk
  goes through Glance as before. Reading a block device requires root or
  membership in the `disk` group.
  ([#271](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/271),
  [#289](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/289),
  [#290](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/290))

## Improvements

- Backups now record the format of the disk, `raw` or `qcow2`, and restores
  accept both.
