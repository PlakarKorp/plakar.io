## New

- The new `openstack_transfer` option sets how disks move between Cinder and
  plakar, for volume backups, volume restores and Nova server backups. `auto`,
  the default, attaches the volume to the Nova server plakar runs on when the
  host allows it, and goes through Glance otherwise. `attach` always attaches,
  and the job fails when it cannot. `glance` always goes through Glance.
  ([#391](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/391))

## Improvements

- Every snapshot, temporary volume, image, server, restored volume and
  attachment the integration creates is logged with its ID and what it was made
  from, so a leftover resource can be traced back to its job.
  ([#425](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/425))

## Fixes

- Attaching a volume no longer fails when the metadata service of the cloud is
  slow to answer.
