## Improvements

- The inventory now exposes the auth URL and region as endpoint attributes.

## Fixes

- Volume backups no longer fail on clouds running Cinder 2025.1 or older, which
  rejected the temporary volume created during the backup.
  ([#286](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/286))
