## New

- Restore accepts `skip_ownership`, `skip_permissions` and `skip_times` to leave
  the ownership, permissions or modification times of restored files unchanged.
- Restore accepts `skip_root_perms_and_time` to leave the permissions and times
  of the restore target directory unchanged, for targets such as mount points
  where they cannot be set.

## Fixes

- Exclusions are applied correctly on Windows.
- The root of a Windows drive, such as `C:\`, is recorded with the correct name.
