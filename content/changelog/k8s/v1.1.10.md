## New

- Support for KubeVirt VMs: back up and restore virtual machines.
- Support for Block PVCs.
- An explicit security group for PVC and CSI operations.
- Restorable resources are filtered to prevent restoring incompatible objects.
- Inventory can now be filtered by namespace.
- KubeVirt VMs are included in the inventory.
- Example RBAC rules are provided.

## Security

- mTLS secures the communication between Plakar and the created pod.
