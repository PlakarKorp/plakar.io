## New

- Added `plakar_secret_provider` to declare a secret provider.
- `plakar_store` and `plakar_connector` take secrets from a secret provider with
  `secret_fields`, or as write-only values with `fields_wo` and
  `fields_wo_version`. Write-only fields require Terraform 1.11 or later.
- Scaleway inventories take their keys from a secret provider, or as write-only
  values.
- Secret providers can read secrets from Scaleway Secret Manager.
- Added `plakar_integration` to install integrations from the catalog.
- Added `plakar_environment` and `plakar_data_class` to manage the environments
  and data classes of an organization.
- `plakar_inventory` synchronizes an inventory after it is created or changed,
  with `sync` and `sync_triggers`.

## Improvements

- A connector whose inventory resource was deleted can now be imported and
  managed.
- Data classes on a store or a destination, and a temperature on a connector,
  are refused at plan time.
- The documentation of `plakar_member` and `plakar_grant` states the role needed
  to manage them.

## Fixes

- Creating a store or a connector that carries a secret works with Plakar
  Control Plane `v0.19.0` and later. Updates keep the secrets they do not send.
- A store whose storage fails to initialize now fails the apply with the reason.

> [!WARNING]+ Upgrading from v0.10.1
>
> If v0.10.1 left a store or a connector tainted, untaint it before the first
> apply with this version. Otherwise Terraform replaces the store, and the
> replacement fails because its storage is already initialized.
>
> ```shell
> terraform untaint plakar_store.example
> terraform untaint plakar_connector.example
> terraform apply
> ```
