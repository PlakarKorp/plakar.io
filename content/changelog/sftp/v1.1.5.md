## New

- The `set_owner` option sets the owner and group of restored files on the
  server.

## Fixes

- On Windows, the integration no longer depends on SSH multiplexing or an
  authentication socket.
- Hardlinks are created correctly on restore.
- Restores no longer fail when creating the restore root.
- The `dont_traverse_fs` check was removed, since it cannot be implemented over
  SFTP.
