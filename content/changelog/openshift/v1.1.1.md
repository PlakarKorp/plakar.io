## New

- The inventory lists the contents of a whole cluster or of a single namespace.
- The `openshift` protocol backs up and restores the manifests of a whole
  cluster or of a single namespace.
- The `openshift+vm` protocol backs up and restores an OpenShift Virtualization
  virtual machine with its disks.
- The `openshift+pvc` protocol streams the contents of a PVC through a data
  mover pod, and restores into a new or an existing PVC.
- The `openshift+csi` protocol takes a CSI `VolumeSnapshot` and reads from it,
  for a consistent capture. Data backed up with it is restored with
  `openshift+pvc`.
- A manifest restore leaves out the objects OpenShift recreates on its own, so
  they are not duplicated or brought back stale: `Build` and `ImageTag` objects,
  build pods and their config maps.

## Security

- The data mover pod requests no extra capabilities. OpenShift runs it with a
  UID from the range of the namespace and grants it only the rights it needs.

## Improvements

- An `openshift+pvc` backup skips the `lost+found` directory at the root of the
  volume.
- An `openshift+pvc` restore does not restore the permissions and timestamps of
  the root directory of the volume, which the non-root data mover pod cannot
  change.
