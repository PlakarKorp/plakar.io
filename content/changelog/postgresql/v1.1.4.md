## Fixes

- An error while writing the temporary certificate or key file from
  `ssl_cert_data`, `ssl_key_data` or `ssl_root_cert_data` is now reported.
  Before, the connection went ahead with a possibly incomplete file.
  ([#477](https://github.com/PlakarKorp/integrations/pull/477))
