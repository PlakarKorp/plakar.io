# Restore Points

The **Restore points** page, under **Governance**, lists every restore point of
the organization, grouped by day, with the source it was backed up from and
every store that holds a copy. It can be filtered by creation date, source,
store and label. From the list, a restore point can be [restored](../restore),
[checked](../check), placed under a [legal hold](../../compliance/legal-hold),
or removed from the stores you pick. A copy under a legal hold is kept when the
others are removed.
