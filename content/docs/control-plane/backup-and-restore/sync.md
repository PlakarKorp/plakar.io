# Syncing Stores

A sync, started from **Backup & restore** > **Sync**, copies all the restore
points of a store, or the recent ones, to another store of any inventory, to
keep a second copy in another location or on another storage tier. Once copied,
a restore point can be restored from either store. A sync takes the same options
as a [sync task](../../scheduling/tasks#sync-task).
