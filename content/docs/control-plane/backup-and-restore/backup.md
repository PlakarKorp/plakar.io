# Running Backups

The **Backup** page, under **Backup & restore**, lists resources with the date
of their last backup and whether an [SLA policy](../../compliance/policies)
covers them. When a policy covers the resource, the store of that policy is
selected. Otherwise, the backup is a one-off and nothing schedules the next one.
A backup takes the same options as a
[backup task](../../scheduling/tasks#backup-task).
