### 2. Severity levels {#annex-2-section-2}

P1 – Critical: backup or restore operations blocked, or production severely
impacted, with no viable workaround.

P2 – High: major functionality degraded, but backup and restore operational and
no immediate data loss.

P3 – Standard: non-blocking issue or degraded functionality without impact on
core operations.

P4 – Low: information request, usage question, or minor cosmetic anomaly.

### 3. Coverage hours {#annex-2-section-3}

Basic SLA Support: Monday to Friday, excluding French public holidays, from 9:30
am to 5:30 pm (Paris time).

Premium SLA Support: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with on-call handling for P1
Incidents.

### 4. Target response times {#annex-2-section-4}

The response time is the period between the Client&#39;s valid Incident report
(in accordance with section 5) and Plakar&#39;s confirmation that the Incident
has been registered and is being investigated. It is not a resolution time.
Response times are counted within the applicable coverage hours (business hours
for Basic; continuously for Premium).

### 5. Incident reporting process {#annex-2-section-5}

Any Incident must be reported to Plakar by the Client by email at
support@plakar.io, or using the email address specified in the Order Form or on
the official support website, with sufficient accuracy and detail to identify
the Incident. Any request made outside of these channels shall not be considered
valid for SLA purposes.

For Basic SLA Support, requests for assistance are made Monday to Friday,
excluding French public holidays, from 9:30 am to 5:30 pm. For Premium SLA
Support, requests for assistance are made at any time, seven days a week.

In response to its notification, the Client receives, without delay, a
time-stamped acknowledgement of receipt. The time stamp is made in Paris time
(CET or CEST). The acknowledgement of receipt is the moment when the Incident is
brought to Plakar&#39;s knowledge and constitutes the starting point for the
applicable response times set out above.

### 6. Workaround {#annex-2-section-6}

While waiting for a definitive solution, Plakar may propose a workaround to the
Client.

### 7. Vendor dependencies {#annex-2-section-7}

Plakar may use third-party vendors for the partial execution of certain features
of the Software. As a result, a vendor&#39;s issue or failure may affect
portions of the Software. When an Incident is diagnosed as originating from a
vendor, Plakar uses its best efforts, working with that vendor, to resolve it.
However, as service and support levels vary from vendor to vendor, the
resolution time of such Incidents is beyond Plakar&#39;s direct control.
